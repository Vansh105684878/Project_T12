Data Dictionary & Glossary

Official metadata reference appendix mapping variables and reporting parameters for the Australian Road Trauma dataset (2011–2021).

Data Field / Term Classification Type Technical Definition & Reporting Scope
Hospitalisation Core Metric Unit An injury event resulting in admission to an Australian hospital facility. This excludes records where the patient was pronounced deceased within 30 days of the crash incident.
Road User Type Dimension Attribute The designated role of the injured entity at the time of impact. Key categories include: Car Driver, Car Passenger, Motorcyclist, Pedal Cyclist, and Pedestrian.
Counterparty Risk Variable The external object, environment, or collision partner involved in the crash vector (e.g., Fixed or stationary object, Non-collision transport accident, Car, pick-up truck or van).
ABS Remoteness Area Geographic Boundary Regional classifications assigned using the Australian Bureau of Statistics remoteness structure, sorting profiles into Major Cities, Regional, or Remote.
Bed Days Volume Metric The cumulative number of total midnight counts spent by a patient inside a hospital ward unit for an injury treatment cycle.
Died_cases Fatality Marker Suppressed or historical attributes tracking cases where mortality outcomes occurred due to complex trauma complications.

Dataset Provenance & Authority

The structural data driving this intelligence system is derived completely from the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare (AIHW) National Hospital Morbidity Database (NHMD).

"Data compiles national hospital configurations capturing extensive clinical matrices, managed under administrative guidelines to monitor injury trends across state and territory networks."
